Iris Support

Iris is a phone app for your own Hermes agent. It needs a Hermes dashboard to talk to, which you run yourself. Most problems are about reaching that server, and the answers are below.

Getting connected

  1. Open Iris and enter the address of your Hermes dashboard: the one you open in a browser, usually on port 9119. A tunnel, Tailscale or a local address on the same network all work.
  2. If the dashboard is published behind Cloudflare Access, Iris asks for a service token (a client ID and a client secret). See the question below.
  3. Sign in with your dashboard username and password.

Questions

Do I need my own server?

Yes. Iris does not include an agent or a hosted service. It is a client for a Hermes dashboard that you run, or that someone you trust runs for you.

It says it could not connect

Check that the address starts with https:// (or http:// for a private network), that the dashboard is running, and that your phone can reach it. Try opening the same address in your phone’s browser. If you are away from home and the server is on your home network, you will need a tunnel or Tailscale. The connection screen in Iris shows whether the link is connecting, live or reconnecting.

What is a Cloudflare Access service token?

If your dashboard is behind Cloudflare Access, the app cannot complete Cloudflare’s browser login, so it uses a service token instead. In Cloudflare Zero Trust, create one under Access → Service credentials, then add a Service Auth policy for it on the application that protects your dashboard. Paste its client ID and secret into Iris. Iris sends the token only over HTTPS.

My server uses a self-signed certificate

Iris can accept a self-signed certificate if you choose to trust it. That trust applies to that one server only.

I see “Reconnecting”

Iris reconnects by itself after a dropped connection, for example when you change networks or the phone sleeps. If it does not recover, check that your server is up.

Can I have more than one agent profile?

Yes, if your Hermes server has several profiles. Tap the profile pill at the top of the home screen to switch. Iris remembers your choice.

How do I archive or rename a conversation?

Press and hold a conversation on the home screen. Archived conversations are under Archived at the bottom of the list, where you can bring them back.

How do I start over or remove my saved login?

Uninstall and reinstall Iris. On an iPhone, a saved login can remain in the Keychain after deletion, so to make it unusable, change that password or revoke that service token on your server.

Where is my data?

Your conversations live on your Hermes server. Iris sends nothing to us. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Your Hermes agent, in your pocket.

Iris is a free, open-source mobile client for your own Hermes dashboard. Your agent, its tools and its history stay on your hardware. Bring a running Hermes server, or explore the built-in offline demo first.

CHAT AND TAKE ACTION
Stream replies, read Markdown and code, follow tool calls, and approve or deny actions. Answer password and authentication prompts when your agent needs you.

KEEP CONVERSATIONS ORGANIZED
Search, pin, rename and archive conversations. Switch agent profiles, branch a conversation, and export a chat as Markdown. Servers that support scheduled jobs and tasks also expose those tools in Iris.

DICTATE OR TALK
Dictate messages and make voice calls on compatible servers. Available speech providers and voices depend on your server. GPT-Live calls send audio directly to OpenAI; other calls use your Hermes server and its configured providers.

CONNECT YOUR WAY
Connect over local Wi-Fi, Tailscale or a protected HTTPS address. Iris supports dashboard authentication and Cloudflare Access service tokens. Public server addresses require HTTPS.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from ten skins with light and dark appearances, large text and reduced-motion support.

NO IRIS ACCOUNT REQUIRED
There is no Iris-hosted relay, advertising or analytics. Credentials are stored in the device keychain. You choose the server and the AI providers it uses; their terms and privacy policies apply.

Iris does not include a hosted agent or AI subscription. A compatible Hermes dashboard is required outside the offline demo. Iris is an independent client and is not affiliated with OpenAI or Nous Research.

Iris · phase 4 · live voice modeThe orb is the microphone.The level strip is gone. One orb shows that Iris is hearing you, thinking, using a tool or speaking, and the three audio states never look alike. Every frame has dark and light, phones are 393×852, and A1, B3 and B6 also appear at 320pt and in reduced motion.
THREE RULES FOR TELLING AUDIO STATES APARTDirection · hearing ripples travel inward, speaking ripples travel outward.Shape · hearing ruffles the edge. Speaking keeps the ring round and fills the middle.Colour · pink hearing, teal speaking, violet thinking, amber tools. Same hues as Phase 2.Time · levels attack in 60 ms and release in 280 ms, so the orb settles in pauses.

O1 · O2The orb’s motion languageO1 is live: each orb animates with a simulated level. Every note names what drives it. O2 removes continuous motion; states then differ by colour, shape and static outlines.
O1 · STATE SHEET · DARK, THEN LIGHT
IdleTIME
Lavender ring breathes on a 4 s cycle, ±2.5% radius. Core drifts ±5%. Nothing audible is happening.
ConnectingTIME
Cyan arc, 30% of the ring, turns at 0.8 rev/s. Hands over to listening on the first audio frame.
Listening · quiet roomMIC FLOOR
Mic open, nobody talking. Pink ring breathes on 5 s; room noise nudges the edge by under 1%. No ripples below the 6% threshold.
Hearing · softMIC LEVEL
Edge lobes reach ~4% of radius, ripples drift inward ≈0.8 /s, core +10%. Attack 60 ms, release 280 ms, so it settles between words.
Hearing · loudMIC LEVEL
Edge to ~14% with a fast shimmer on top, ripples inward ≈1.8 /s, ring swells 8%, core +25%. Falls back in 280 ms at a pause.
ThinkingTIME
Three violet arcs turn at 0.4 rev/s, core pulses at 1.6 Hz. Ignores mic and playback. An elapsed timer joins it after 10 s.
Running a toolTIME
Six amber segments at 0.2 rev/s, one satellite dot orbits faster. Tool name and argument sit under the orb in mono.
Speaking · softPLAYBACK LEVEL
Teal. The ring stays round; a filled bloom swells inside it with output level; ripples travel outward ≈0.6 /s.
Speaking · loudPLAYBACK LEVEL
Bloom fills most of the ring, core +40%, ripples outward ≈1.2 /s, glow brightens. Still round, which is the difference from hearing.
InterruptedEVENT
Playback cuts in 120 ms. Ring squashes 10% and flashes white, colour crosses teal to pink in 250 ms, then hearing takes over. Loops every 3.6 s here.
MutedSTATE
Grey ring with a slash, core dimmed to 60%. Ignores the mic completely, so nothing moves.
ErrorEVENT
Red ring with an open gap. One 300 ms shake, then still. Held until the user acts.
Needs youTIME
Two orange arcs turn at 0.06 rev/s, core pulses at 0.9 Hz. Waits for a tap or a spoken answer.
ReconnectingTIME
Twelve short grey segments drift at 0.1 rev/s, core flickers softly. Call is paused, not lost.
